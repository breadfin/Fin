What Happens If You Don't Cancel Your Loan?

If an approved or active loan application is not canceled in a timely manner through our official portal, it leads to direct financial, credit score, and administrative recovery impacts.

IMPACT 1

Unwanted EMI & Interest Debits

Active loans trigger scheduled monthly automated EMI deductions. Compounding interest accumulates, increasing your total debt burden unnecessarily.

❌ Result: Non-refundable interest outflows and NACH bank penalties.
IMPACT 2

Severe CIBIL Score Degradation

Unserviced active loans are reported straight to RBI-licensed credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian), causing rapid credit score drops of 80 to 150 points.

⚠️ Result: Future rejections for home loans, vehicle loans, and credit cards.
IMPACT 3

NBFC Recovery & Legal Notices

Failing to service active loan mandates generates formal registered legal notices, collection agency proceedings, and Section 138/25 reminders.

🚨 Result: Official collection notices and administrative disputes.
